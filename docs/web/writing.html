Why it is shown this way
· Provider paste reports and clipboard matches are separate evidence classes.
· Full clipboard coverage cannot rule out typing, file loading or other routes.
· Every qualifying copy is retained. Two matching copies mean ambiguous source candidates.
· Evidence applies only to its cited message revision. Late copies trigger a new evaluation; edits invalidate old coordinates.
