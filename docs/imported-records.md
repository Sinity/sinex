# Journal and history inputs

`hledger` discovers `LEDGER_FILE`, `~/.hledger.journal`, and the host's declared `finance` roots. A root can name a journal or a directory of `.journal`, `.hledger` and `journal_clean` files. `text-history` discovers the host's `input:text-history` files. Both also accept `--read FORMAT=PATH`.

Journal files use the shared export reader and the source's block framing. Each local include becomes another ordinary file binding. Discovery refreshes the include plan, and each child's reader observes its own changes. Include paths are relative to their parent, absolute, or home-relative (`~/`), with `journal:` prefixes and file globs. Include records name their targets and report missing files, cycles and unsupported targets. Repeated references keep separate identities, including their parent include path. Commands, shell expansion and remote imports are never executed. Native timeclock and timedot includes are kept as `journal.imported` records with their written format and text. Capture does not generate accounting transactions from those records.

`transaction.posted` records keep authored dates with dash, slash or dot separators, effective dates, the default `Y` year, status, code, payee, narration, comments and ordered postings. Partial dates stay partial; resolved civil dates are supplied only when the year is known. Civil dates do not invent an original instant. Postings keep implicit amounts absent and quantities as exact written text, including digit groups and decimal marks. Quoted commodities, posting status, virtual accounts, unit and total prices (`@`, `@@`, `(@)`, `(@@)`), and balance assertions or assignments (`=`, `==`, `=*`, `==*`) are preserved, including prices written on assertions or assignments. Posting annotations keep lot costs, dates, notes and valuation expressions as written text without evaluating them. Capture neither balances transactions nor generates postings from rules.

`journal.directive` records keep the complete text of account/commodity declarations, market prices, defaults, aliases, automatic and periodic rules, other native directives, ignored Ledger declarations and Python blocks, and comments. Included files inherit the authored year at their include. Other directives remain written records rather than evaluated accounting settings.

Identity includes host, journal, archive member path and duplicate number, complete block digest, duplicate number within the member, the parent include path, and resolved civil dates. Changing a parent year reads an unchanged child again under its new authored context. Fully dated transactions keep their identity when an unused default year changes. Distinct archive members can reorder without changing keys. Duplicate counts cross part boundaries and reset at a new member or export revision. The parser keeps one counter per distinct block to distinguish equal transactions even when other records separate them. The focused carry measurement checks 105, 726, 6,936 and 69,036 serialized bytes at 1, 10, 100 and 1,000 distinct records respectively. Carry grows linearly and has no record limit.

Text history reads complete appended lines as `command.imported`. It removes only LF or CRLF; spaces, backslashes and timestamp-looking text stay command content. Empty lines emit no event; whitespace-only lines are commands. Starts and durations stay unknown. Equal commands have distinct record locations. The shared append reader handles appends, rotation and restart within its remembered tail.

These sources declare guarded defaults.
